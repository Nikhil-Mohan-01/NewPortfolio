## Goal

Drive a bounded reversible route with native scroll, open content from a redesigned launcher, and explore three employment landmarks on the right-hand curb.

## Findings and decisions

The complete portfolio branch contains the current cockpit and readable routes. Main is still behind those prerequisites. The existing camera looks down −Z and world +X is therefore the right side. The old street wrapped a finite pool with elapsed-time movement. Keep its materials and seeded geometry, but extend the logical sequence to cover a bounded absolute route. One sticky cockpit uses real document scrolling. Lock that scroll for dashboard and detail states.
